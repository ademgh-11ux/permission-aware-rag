# AdemPro Employee Handbook

This handbook is for AdemPro employees only. Do not share it with customers or partners.

## Working hours

Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00, with a one-hour lunch break. Teams may agree on flexible hours with their manager, as long as everyone is available for core hours from 10:00 to 16:00.

## Hybrid work

Employees can work from home up to **2 days per week**. Tuesdays and Thursdays are office days for everyone, so teams can meet in person. Remote work from outside Malaysia requires written approval from your manager and HR.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop and an AdemPro SmartHub Lite (AP-4470) for testing at home. Equipment remains company property and must be returned on the last working day.

## IT security

- Use the company password manager for all work accounts.
- Turn on two-factor authentication for email, AdemPro Desk admin, and GitHub.
- Never paste customer data or internal documents into external AI tools that are not on the approved tools list.
- Report suspicious emails to security@adempro.com. Do not click links in them.

## Customer data

Customer data can only be accessed for a specific support or engineering task. Access is logged and reviewed every month. Sharing customer data outside AdemPro systems is a serious disciplinary offense.

## Expense claims

Submit expense claims through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense, with a receipt attached. Claims above MYR 500 need manager approval before the expense is made.

## Internal discounts

Employees get a 40% discount on AdemPro hardware, limited to 3 devices per year. Use the internal discount code from the HR portal at checkout.

## Who to contact

- IT problems: it-help@adempro.com
- HR questions: hr@adempro.com
- Security incidents: security@adempro.com
