# AdemPro Product Catalog

AdemPro builds smart office devices and the AdemPro Desk platform that connects them. All prices are in Malaysian Ringgit (MYR) and include SST.

### SmartHub Pro (AP-4471)

The SmartHub Pro is AdemPro's flagship hub. It supports Wi-Fi 6 and Zigbee 3.0, includes a backup battery that lasts up to 4 hours during power cuts, and receives automatic firmware updates. It is recommended for offices with more than 15 employees.

### SmartHub Lite (AP-4470)

The SmartHub Lite covers the same core features as the Pro but supports fewer devices and has no backup battery. It is a good fit for small offices and home offices.

## Software

### AdemPro Desk

AdemPro Desk is the web and mobile platform for managing devices, booking desks and rooms, and viewing occupancy analytics.

| Plan | Price (MYR) | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 49 per month | Up to 10 devices, desk booking, basic analytics |
| Business | 149 per month | Up to 100 devices, room booking, advanced analytics, API access |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | Unlimited devices, single sign-on (SSO), dedicated support |

Annual billing gives a 15% discount on Starter and Business plans.

## Warranty

All AdemPro hardware includes a manufacturer's warranty against defects, with the length listed in the table above. The warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage, or unauthorized repairs. To make a warranty claim, contact support@adempro.com with your product code and serial number.

## Compatibility

All sensors and panels require either a SmartHub Pro (AP-4471) or a SmartHub Lite (AP-4470). Devices cannot connect to AdemPro Desk directly.
