# Customer FAQ

## Orders and shipping

### How long does delivery take?

Orders within Peninsular Malaysia arrive in 2 to 4 business days. Orders to Sabah and Sarawak take 5 to 7 business days. AdemPro currently ships only within Malaysia and Singapore.

### Is shipping free?

Shipping is free for orders above MYR 300. Orders below MYR 300 have a flat shipping fee of MYR 15.

### Can I change or cancel my order?

You can change or cancel an order until it ships. Once you receive a shipping confirmation email, the order can no longer be changed, but you can return it under the Refund and Return Policy.

## Setup and devices

### How do I set up my SmartHub?

1. Plug in the hub and wait for the light to turn solid blue.
2. Download the AdemPro Desk app and sign in.
3. Tap "Add hub" and scan the QR code on the bottom of the hub.

Setup usually takes less than 5 minutes.

### My sensor is not showing up in the app. What should I do?

First, make sure the sensor is within 15 meters of the hub. Then hold the sensor's reset button for 10 seconds until the light blinks orange, and add it again from the app. If it still does not appear, restart the hub.

### What does a red light on the hub mean?

A solid red light means the hub has lost its internet connection. A blinking red light means a firmware update failed. In that case, unplug the hub for 30 seconds and plug it back in. It will retry the update automatically.

## Account and billing

### How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password" on the AdemPro Desk sign-in page and follow the link sent to your email. Reset links expire after 1 hour.

### Which payment methods do you accept?

AdemPro accepts Visa, Mastercard, FPX online banking, and Touch 'n Go eWallet. Enterprise customers can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice.

## Warranty and support

### How long is the warranty?

Most AdemPro devices come with a 12-month warranty. Some premium devices have longer coverage; check the Product Catalog for the warranty on a specific product.

### How do I contact support?

You can reach support through the chat on adempro.com, by email at support@adempro.com, or by phone at +60 3-7700 4471 from Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 Malaysia time.
