# Leave and Compensation Policy

Confidential. Access is restricted to the HR team.

## Annual leave

| Years of service | Annual leave days |
|---|---|
| Less than 2 years | 14 days |
| 2 to 5 years | 18 days |
| More than 5 years | 22 days |

Up to 5 unused leave days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees are entitled to 14 days of paid sick leave per year, or 60 days if hospitalization is required. A medical certificate is needed for every sick day.

## Parental leave

- Maternity leave: 98 days, fully paid.
- Paternity leave: 7 days, fully paid.

## Salary bands (engineering)

| Level | Title | Monthly salary range (MYR) |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Junior Software Engineer | 5,000 to 7,000 |
| E2 | Software Engineer | 7,000 to 10,500 |
| E3 | Senior Software Engineer | 10,500 to 15,000 |
| E4 | Staff Engineer | 15,000 to 21,000 |

## Performance bonus

Bonuses are paid once a year in February. The target bonus is 1 month of salary for E1 and E2, and 2 months for E3 and E4, adjusted by individual and company performance.

## Salary reviews

Salary reviews happen every January. Out-of-cycle raises require approval from the department head and the HR director.
