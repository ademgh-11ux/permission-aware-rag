# Refund and Return Policy

AdemPro wants every customer to be happy with their purchase. This policy explains when and how you can return a product or get a refund.

## Who this policy covers

This policy applies to all hardware bought directly from AdemPro (adempro.com or AdemPro stores) and to AdemPro Desk software subscriptions. Products bought from resellers follow the reseller's own policy.

## Hardware returns

You can return any hardware product within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund, as long as:

- The product is in its original packaging.
- All accessories, cables, and manuals are included.
- The device has not been physically damaged.

Opened products are accepted. A device that has been set up and used is fine, as long as it is undamaged and factory reset.

### Return conditions at a glance

| Situation | Within 30 days | After 30 days |
|---|---|---|
| Unopened product | Full refund | Not accepted |
| Opened, undamaged | Full refund | Not accepted |
| Defective product | Full refund or replacement | Covered by warranty |
| Physically damaged by customer | Not accepted | Not accepted |

### Final sale items

Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page, including clearance stock and refurbished units, cannot be returned unless they are defective.

## Software subscription refunds

AdemPro Desk subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from the account settings page.

- **Monthly plans:** cancellation stops the next renewal. The current month is not refunded.
- **Annual plans:** you can request a prorated refund within **14 days** of purchase or renewal. After 14 days, the plan stays active until the end of the term with no refund.

## How to request a return

1. Contact support at support@adempro.com or through the chat on adempro.com, with your order number.
2. Support will send you a Return Authorization (RA) number within 2 business days.
3. Ship the product with the RA number written on the package. AdemPro pays return shipping for defective products. For other returns, the customer pays return shipping.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **7 business days** after AdemPro receives and inspects the returned product. Bank processing can take an additional 3 to 5 business days.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@adempro.com.
